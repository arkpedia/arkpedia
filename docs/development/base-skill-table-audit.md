## Operators with alternate forms

An operator with an alternate (W and Wiš'adel, Exusiai and Exusiai the New Covenant: the game's `char_meta_table` families) counts exactly the forms the skill text tags. A glossary term around the name (`<$cc.angel>Exusiai</>`) means every operator the term lists (`gamedata_const`, "Includes the following Operators"); a plain tag (`<@cc.kw>W</>`) means that single form, by the string written, so "W" is W alone and "SilverAsh the Reignfrost" the alternate alone. Never by name likeness. The EN and CN texts are read side by side and CN decides where they differ; the build (`scripts/build/base-skill-forms.ts`) refuses a term on a condition that cannot carry forms.
