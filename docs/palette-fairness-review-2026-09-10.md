# Targeted palette review — 2026-09-10

Screened all **816** stored Base/E2 palettes for repeated dark swatches and large Base/E2 skin-color differences. Visually reviewed **43** artworks, including the previously discussed examples and unchanged reference palettes. Corrected **16 palettes / 19 swatches** and retained **27** reviewed palettes. This is a targeted refinement, **not a new visual approval of all 816 artworks**.

## Selection and decisions

The screening found six palettes with at least three swatches below CIELAB lightness 25. These and the 24 largest CIELAB differences between Base/E2 fourth-slot skin colors were added to the nine paired reference operators (overlaps removed). Numerical flags selected candidates only; no color was automatically changed. Base and E2 remain separate, and monochrome clothing and broad scene-light casts are retained when supported by the artwork.

Clothing slots should cover distinct recognizable garments where possible. A replacement must represent a broad part of the named feature rather than an isolated highlight, deep shadow, or unrelated effect. E2 effects may supply the accent slot. Narrow exposed skin gaps alone do not require slot four to be skin.

All 26 palettes with prior user acceptances or approval were left unchanged, including Harold Base. Every changed record archives its complete previous decision. New swatches store the exact opaque source pixel and feature label; retained swatches do not claim new sampling. No source illustrations, release filters, game rules, or provisional fallback recipes were changed.
