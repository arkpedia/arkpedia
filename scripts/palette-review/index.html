    How to choose the five colors
    1. Hair: a representative hair color. Use visible headgear, fur, or shell when hair is covered or absent.
    2. Clothing 1: the main clothing color.
    3. Clothing 2: a second recognizable clothing color.
    4. Skin / clothing 3: use skin when it forms a substantial visible part of the artwork; otherwise choose another clothing color. A small face or a pair of hands alone does not require a skin swatch.
    5. Accent / item: a distinctive weapon, accessory, trim, or effect, even if it takes up little space.

    Choose colors from visible features, avoiding isolated highlights or deep shadows. For nonhuman operators, use body or equipment colors in the clothing slots. Review Base and Elite 2 separately; a monochrome design can stay monochrome.
